# Step 3a: Record yourself (10 minutes)

You record the performance. The model replaces you with the character and **keeps your movement, timing and speed**. A good recording is 80% of a believable result.

## Setup

| Do | Why |
|---|---|
| **Phone, vertical (9:16), 1080p or higher** | Social video is vertical. Higher resolution gives the model more detail |
| **Camera at eye height, locked, chest-up** | The model keeps your framing |
| **Sit or stand so your head is in the middle to lower part of the frame** | Leaves empty space on top. You will put headlines there |
| **One plain background, even light** | Less clutter means fewer artifacts |
| **Keep both hands inside the frame while you gesture** | Hands are what make it look human. A hand that leaves the frame gets lost |
| **Plain clothes** | The outfit is replaced, but a plain one tracks better than a busy one |

## Read your script without looking down

The model copies your head angle. If you read from a screen below the lens, the character looks down and loses the audience.

- Put the script **right next to or above the lens**, or use a teleprompter app
- Speak the lines at your **natural speed**, with natural pauses. The pauses become your cut points
- Gesture on the important words

The prompt in the next step also forces the character to look into the camera, but a good source video helps more than any prompt.

## Cut your video into clips of 30 seconds or less

The motion transfer limit is about 30 seconds per clip.

- Cut at the **end of a sentence**, never mid-sentence
- Put a short pause at the end of each clip
- **Trim each clip to a whole number of seconds** (for example exactly 26.0s, not 26.9s). In our test, both exports came back rounded **down** to a whole second and lost the last 0.9s of speech. If you trim to a whole second yourself, nothing is lost
- Keep the **original audio files**. You will use your real voice in the final video, not the generated one

## Quick check before uploading

- [ ] Each clip is 30s or less and a whole number of seconds long
- [ ] Both hands are visible most of the time
- [ ] Your eyes are looking at (or close to) the lens
- [ ] No one else is in the frame
